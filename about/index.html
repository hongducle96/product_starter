About

Your first step into Product.

Product Starter helps beginners understand Product, learn it day by day, and grow a career in it.

Most Product content is written by experienced PMs for other experienced PMs. It is full of frameworks and acronyms, and it assumes you already know the basics. Product Starter starts one step earlier: if a curious 12-year-old could not follow it, we rewrite it.

Every lesson uses one everyday example, a toy car, a bakery, a shopping app, and ends with one thing to remember. No hype, no shortcuts, just the path, one day at a time.
