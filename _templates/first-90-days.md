text: |
  FIRST 90 DAYS

  Days 1–30: Listen
    - Meet every person on the team 1:1
    - Talk to 10 users
    - Read the last 3 months of support tickets

  Days 31–60: Learn
    - Map the product and its key metrics
    - Write down the top 3 problems you see

  Days 61–90: Improve
    - Ship one small, visible improvement
    - Share what you learned with the team
