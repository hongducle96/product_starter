---
title: "User interview script"
desc: "Open questions that get stories, not opinions."
format: Doc
icon: story
order: 2
text: |
  USER INTERVIEW SCRIPT (30 min)

  1. Warm up (5 min)
    - Tell me a little about your day-to-day.

  2. The last time (15 min)
    - Tell me about the last time you [did the task].
    - What happened just before that?
    - What was hardest about it?
    - What did you try? How did it go?

  3. Dig deeper (7 min)
    - Why was that important to you?
    - What would have made it easier?

  4. Wrap up (3 min)
    - Is there anything I should have asked?

  Rules: ask about the past, not the future. Never pitch your idea.
